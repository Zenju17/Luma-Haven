# Audit and governance

The existing audit table is mutable and sparse; no append-only guarantee exists. Implement a separate immutable event stream with UUID, UTC timestamp, actor and actor type, organization/property, action, resource ID/type, correlation ID, outcome, minimal change metadata and optional reason. Protect update/delete through a restricted runtime role and separately controlled archival role; test both ORM and direct SQL. Cover login and failures, staff and permission changes, configuration, reservations, check-in/out, payment/refund, folio adjustment, operations, guest access/export and denied access. Current coverage is limited to the events listed in the assessment.

Set retention periods with legal and business owners before deployment; no legal period is assumed. Limit audit access to authorized investigators, log exports, encrypt archives, minimize PII, avoid credentials, OTPs and payment data, and document incident chain of custody. Define guest access/export, correction and anonymization procedures, preserving financial and audit obligations. OTP and expired quote/hold retention should be short and scheduled; outbox, webhook payload, guest, cancellation, completed housekeeping and maintenance retention require approved policies. No automatic deletion is introduced here.
